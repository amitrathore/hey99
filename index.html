The best local recommendations already happen every day: a friend names a repair shop, a creator shows a favorite spot, a neighbor connects a business to its next customer.

Your way in

Four ways to move
the market.

Start with the contribution you already know how to make.

01 / CONNECT↗

Bring in sellers

Introduce local merchants, independent sellers and brands whose offers belong here. Eligible sales can keep rewarding the person who made the introduction.

02 / GROW✳

Invite cosellers

Bring in people who know how to make a useful recommendation. When they earn through eligible coselling, a recruiter share can flow to you.

03 / CREATE✎

Tell the story

Make honest photos, videos and stories that help people understand a local offer. Content used in attributed coselling can earn a creator share.

04 / SHARE➜

Pass it along

Share offers with people who may value them. Eligible sales can reward the chain of people whose recommendations helped a buyer arrive.

Exact rates depend on each live offer. No reward is earned from sharing or inviting alone.
