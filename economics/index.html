#hey99 · How rewards work

Local commerce.
Shared opportunity.

A local business brings something useful. A neighbor makes an introduction. Someone shares a story. Together, they help good things find the people who need them. This is an open local market economy: businesses and everyday people building opportunity together, and sharing the value they help create.

Everyday connections, shared opportunity

You already have
something to contribute.

You may already recommend a neighborhood shop, share a recipe or tell a friend about a local maker. Here, those everyday contributions can help businesses grow—and become a side income when they lead to qualifying purchases.

We call this coselling: helping commerce happen through connections, content and recommendations. A coseller is anyone who contributes in these ways. You don’t need to be a salesperson, have a large audience or close a sale yourself.

Start with what you enjoy. Introduce a business you believe in, connect a friend, make a useful video or share something worth discovering. You can take part in your own way, at your own pace.

A recommendation can be the beginning

Help a good thing
find its people.

A purchase can begin with one person’s introduction, grow through someone else’s story and happen after another recommendation. We call these connected contributions the chain of influence.

#coselling is #noselling. You don’t have to pitch, persuade or close a sale. Your contribution can begin someone’s journey, rather than be the final step before they buy.

Imagine your mom shares a recipe she liked from the market on Nextdoor. Someone follows her recommendation. Later, that same person discovers a different article through Facebook and buys a travel package. If both interactions qualify under that offer’s rules, your mom and the other contributor automatically share the available commission—even though she never mentioned the trip or closed the sale.

The platform connects the qualifying steps in that buyer’s journey and shares the reward automatically. The influence window is simply the period before a purchase during which helpful interactions can count. The offer explains which interactions qualify and how the reward is shared.

It starts with a local business

Your business.
Your offer.

Merchants, sellers and brands reach nearby customers through trusted recommendations. You approve your offer, agree to its commission and state the eligible value: the whole retail transaction or a clearly defined portion of it.

That commission funds the coseller reward pool—the amount available to reward the people helping commerce happen. Seller-approved offers can put up to 30% of eligible transaction value, and sometimes more, into this pool.

Each offer publishes its actual rate, eligible value, processing fees, exclusions and reward rules. That gives businesses control over their offers and contributors a clear picture of the opportunity.

Active participation, lasting relationships

Find your way
to take part.

Some contributions happen today. Others keep working over time. You can choose one path or combine several.

01 / CONNECT

Refer a seller or brand

Introduce a merchant, seller or brand through an accepted, credited referral. You receive 5% of that seller’s eligible coseller pool as their qualifying offers sell. This share is reserved before the remaining pool is shared among the people influencing the purchase.

Set up the relationship once. It does not expire and can keep generating passive income for as long as the platform exists, without making the introduction again. Your payments follow the seller’s qualifying sales.

02 / GROW

Refer another participant

Know someone who enjoys connecting people, creating content or recommending useful things? Introduce them as a coseller through an accepted, credited referral. You receive 5% of their eligible coseller earnings.

This relationship also does not expire: it can generate passive income for as long as the platform exists, whenever that person earns qualifying rewards. Referral chains follow the offer’s rules; you don’t need to take part in each purchase yourself.

03 / CREATE

Create something useful

Make photos, videos, recipes or stories that other cosellers use in qualifying sales. You receive 10% of that coseller’s earnings from the sale, allocated from their reward.

Useful content can keep working for you. When others reuse it in qualifying sales, it can generate income again—without creating something new each time. The creator share adds no extra charge for the seller or buyer.

04 / SHARE

Share a discovery

Recommend an offer or share useful content with people who might appreciate it. This is an active way to help local commerce grow—and earn from purchases your contribution helps make possible.

If yours is the only qualifying engagement in the influence window, you can receive the whole available coseller pool, adjusted for applicable passive referral and creator shares. If several credited contributors influence that buyer, you receive a portion of the available pool, shared automatically under the offer’s rules.

That pool can be up to 30% of the offer’s eligible value, or higher when the seller approves it. Your contribution need not be the final step before purchase; rewards follow qualifying sales, rather than sharing alone.

Clarity builds trust

Clear terms.
Shared confidence.

You can combine introductions, content and recommendations to build a side income in a way that fits your life. Referral relationships can keep earning without repeated introductions, and useful content can keep earning when reused.

Before you participate, each offer shows its eligible value, reward pool, applicable allocations and influence window. A percentage always has a stated base, so you can see what your share means.

Your eligible contribution earns the share stated in the offer. Only a cancelled or reversed sale reduces the earnings tied to that sale. Reversals include refunds and chargebacks. An open dispute may temporarily pause payment while it’s resolved.

Rewards become payable once the sale meets the published completion and settlement conditions. Seller referrals and creator rewards are planned for commerce activation; preview activity does not accrue earnings.
