It starts with a local business

The seller sets
the offer.

Your business. Your offer. Clear terms.

Merchants, sellers and brands can reach nearby customers through local stories and trusted recommendations. You approve the offer, agree to its commission and define the eligible value: the whole retail transaction or a stated portion of it.

The commission funds a coseller reward pool—the amount available to reward the people helping customers discover an offer. A coseller is someone whose recommendation contributes to an eligible sale.

For some offers, that pool can reach 30% of the eligible value or more when the seller approves it. This is an offer-specific pool, not a universal rate or a guaranteed payment to one person.

Published terms identify the actual pool, eligible value, exclusions and attribution rules. Processing fees and offer exclusions affect the distributable amount.

Four ways to contribute

Connections make
commerce happen.

Bring your local knowledge, content or recommendations. When qualifying commerce begins, these contributions can help you build a side income.

01 / CONNECT

Refer a seller or brand

Bring an eligible merchant, seller or brand into the market. You receive an ongoing 5% of that seller’s eligible coseller pool as their qualifying offers sell.

This share is reserved from the pool before the remaining balance is split among the chain of influence. The introduction can continue to support you as qualifying sales happen.

02 / GROW

Refer a coseller

Introduce someone who helps sell. When that person earns an eligible coseller reward, you receive a 5% recruiter override on their earnings.

The shared model supports recruiter chains, subject to each offer’s rules and eligibility. The ongoing share depends on qualifying activity.

03 / CREATE

Create useful content

Make photos, videos or stories that another coseller uses in an attributed sale. You receive 10% of that coseller’s earning from the sale.

The creator share comes from that coseller’s reward; it is not an extra charge to the seller or buyer. Useful content can keep contributing across qualifying sales.

04 / SHARE

Recommend an offer

Connect an offer with people who may value it. An eligible sale can reward the chain of influence—the people whose recommendations helped the buyer arrive.

Your share depends on the offer’s pool and the eligible contributions in that chain. Sharing alone creates no payout, and the whole pool does not go to one person.

Clarity builds trust

Clear terms
before a sale.

A percentage needs a base.

Each offer must identify its eligible transaction value or portion, seller commission, coseller pool, exclusions and attribution window. A 30% pool means 30% of that stated eligible value when the offer supports it.

Referrals, recommendations and content shares depend on qualifying activity and an attributed sale. A sale must be completed and survive the relevant return, refund and dispute conditions before rewards can settle.

Refunds, returns, disputes or ineligible activity can reduce or cancel a reward. Seller recruitment and content-share payouts are planned for commerce activation; they are not operating on preview pages.
